# OctoGuide 0.5.9

Find step no longer places starter characters beyond independent unfinished starter quests merely because a later quest, level or XP gives a better placement score. Applicable starter chapters are checked together below level six. Earlier unfinished starter actions in the selected chapter and applicable training before the proposed cursor are retained. Confirmed turn-ins, actual learned spells and explicit Done/Skip choices still count; level alone does not prove training.

Tests reproduce a later active quest winning over an untouched boar quest, an unlearned skill and an untouched scorpion quest. The corrected placement preserves each in sequence and advances to the later quest when the earlier requirements are actually fulfilled. Existing local-objective placement, progress, mode, Back and startup checks pass.

This is a source-verified placement correction. The family's reported characters still need an in-game check. The 0.5.8 startup protection is retained. OctoGuide remains free; no additional DLL or installer is required.
