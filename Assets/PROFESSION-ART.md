# Profession artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-14 for OctoGuide.
The PNG originals are kept in the development deliverables; the addon uses
uncompressed, power-of-two TGA textures for WoW 1.12 compatibility.

ProfessionBanner.tga: 1024 x 256 RGB decorative banner.
Prompt: A horizontal hand-painted early-2000s fantasy MMORPG UI still life of a
blacksmith hammer/anvil, mining pick, green herbs, red alchemy flask, cloth spool
and fishing float on dark navy leather with restrained antique gold trim;
objects at left/right, quiet center, no text/logos/buttons/characters.

ProfessionMark.tga: 64 x 64 RGBA map pin.
Prompt: A compact antique-gold map-pin emblem with an embossed crossed hammer
and leaf, round head and short downward point; hand-painted game UI, dark
outlines, readable at 32 pixels, centered square composition, transparent
background and margin, no text/logos/watermarks/other objects.

Labels, prices, directions and controls are rendered by the addon, not baked
into the generated artwork.
